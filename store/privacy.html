ListFill Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

ListFill is an independent Chrome extension that helps Meesho suppliers save product variants and autofill the Add Product form. It is not affiliated with Meesho.

Data we store

Saved variants, field values, license status, and theme preference are stored in Chrome local storage on your device. We do not upload listing data to our servers.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your Meesho password. We do not sell personal data. Autofill only writes into the form in your browser; you submit the catalog yourself.

Payments

Paid plans are billed by the payment provider you use (for example Razorpay or a checkout link). After payment, a license key is issued so Autofill can continue. Payment processors handle card or UPI details — ListFill does not store card numbers.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer listed on the Chrome Web Store item.
