ListFill Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

ListFill is an independent Chrome extension that helps sellers save product variants and autofill Meesho and Flipkart listing forms. It is not affiliated with Meesho or Flipkart.

Data we store

Saved variants, field values, license status, and theme preference are stored in Chrome local storage on your device. We do not upload listing data to our servers.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your Meesho or Flipkart password. We do not sell personal data. Autofill only writes into the form in your browser; you submit the catalog yourself.

Payments

Paid plans are arranged directly with the developer. After you receive a license key, Autofill can continue. ListFill does not collect card or UPI details.
